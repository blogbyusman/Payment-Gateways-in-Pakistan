Best International Payment Gateway in Pakistan (2026 Guide)

A complete guide to receiving money from abroad — whether you are a freelancer, startup, or online business in Pakistan.

📅 May 2026 ⏱ 8 min read 📍 Pakistan
If you are a freelancer, business owner, or developer in Pakistan trying to receive money from international clients — you already know the struggle. Getting paid from abroad is not as simple as it sounds. But in 2026, the options are better than ever.

In this guide, we cover the best international payment gateways in Pakistan, how they work, and which one is right for you. Whether you want to accept credit cards, PayPal transfers, or bank wires — there is a solution here for you.

Why Pakistan Faces Payment Challenges

Pakistan's financial system has historically been cut off from many global payment platforms. PayPal, for example, does not allow Pakistani users to receive payments. This forced millions of freelancers and small businesses to look for alternatives.

The good news? In recent years, Pakistan's fintech ecosystem has grown rapidly. The State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) has opened new doors, and several local and international payment gateways now work in Pakistan.

List of Payment Gateways in Pakistan

Here is a curated international payment gateway list that actually works for Pakistani businesses and freelancers:

💰

1. Payoneer

Best for Freelancers Global Transfers Low Fees

Payoneer is the most popular international payment method in Pakistan. It lets you receive payments from clients, marketplaces like Upwork and Fiverr, and businesses worldwide. You can withdraw directly to your Pakistani bank account. Setup is free and approval is quick.

📄

2. Stripe (via Third-Party)

For Developers E-commerce

Stripe is not directly available in Pakistan, but many Pakistani businesses use it through a US LLC or via partners. It supports credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Best for SaaS products and online stores targeting international customers.

🌐

3. 2Checkout (now Verifone)

Accepts Pakistan Cards & PayPal

2Checkout officially supports Pakistan. You can accept credit cards, PayPal, and local bank transfers through this platform. It is a strong choice for software companies and digital product sellers looking for international online payment methods in Pakistan.

🔁

4. Wise (formerly TransferWise)

International Transfers Best Exchange Rate

Wise is excellent for receiving international bank transfers at real exchange rates. Pakistani users can use Wise to receive money from abroad and convert to PKR with minimal fees. Sending and receiving in multiple currencies is supported.

📷

5. JazzCash & EasyPaisa

Local Gateway Mobile Wallet

JazzCash and EasyPaisa are Pakistan's most popular mobile wallets. While primarily used for local transactions, they now support international remittances. Businesses can integrate these as checkout options for Pakistani customers.

📱

6. NayaPay & SadaPay

New Fintechs Freelancer-Friendly

These newer Pakistani fintech apps now offer freelancer accounts that can receive international payments, especially from platforms like Upwork. They come with Mastercard-linked accounts, making international payments in Pakistan more accessible than ever.

💳

7. Skrill

Available in Pakistan Freelancers & Traders

Skrill is a global digital wallet that works in Pakistan. Unlike PayPal, Skrill allows Pakistani users to receive and send money. It is commonly used by freelancers, affiliate marketers, and online traders.

How to Get International Payments in Pakistan

Here is a simple step-by-step path to start receiving money from abroad:

  1. Pick a payment gateway from the list above. Payoneer is the easiest to start with.
  2. Create an account and verify your identity with your CNIC and bank details.
  3. Share your payment link or virtual bank account details with your international client.
  4. Once the payment arrives, withdraw it to your local bank in PKR.
  5. Keep records for FBR tax purposes — international income is taxable in Pakistan.
💡

Pro Tip: If you are a freelancer registered on Upwork, Fiverr, or Toptal — Payoneer connects directly to these platforms. You do not even need a client to send a manual payment link. The platform handles it automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does PayPal work in Pakistan?

No. As of 2026, PayPal does not officially support receiving payments in Pakistan. You can use Payoneer, Skrill, or Wise as strong alternatives.

What is the best international payment gateway for freelancers in Pakistan?

Payoneer is the most widely used and trusted option. It connects with almost every major freelance marketplace and allows direct bank withdrawals in Pakistan.

Is it legal to receive international payments in Pakistan?

Yes, it is 100% legal. You simply need to declare your income and follow State Bank of Pakistan regulations for foreign remittances. Many freelancers and agencies do this every day.

Can I use Stripe in Pakistan?

Not directly. But you can use Stripe if you register a business entity abroad (such as a US LLC) and link it to a US bank account. Many Pakistani tech entrepreneurs do this successfully.

Which payment gateway has the lowest fees in Pakistan?

Wise has some of the lowest fees for bank-to-bank international transfers. For freelance platforms, Payoneer typically charges around 2%, which is competitive for the region.

Final Thoughts

The international payments landscape in Pakistan has improved a lot. Freelancers, startups, and online businesses now have multiple solid options to receive money from clients anywhere in the world.

Start with Payoneer if you are new — it is the safest and simplest choice. As your business grows, you can explore 2Checkout for accepting cards on your website, or Wise for cheap international bank transfers.

The key is to not wait. Set up your account today and start receiving what you have earned.
